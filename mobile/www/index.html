Staff app — first-time setup
只需設定一次，之後打開 App 會直接到登入頁。
Set once — afterwards the app opens straight at the sign-in page.

正式網站必須是 https://。測試時（同一 Wi-Fi）可用 http://192.168.x.x:8000。
The live site must be https://. For testing on the same Wi-Fi, http://192.168.x.x:8000 also works.

要更換網址：在登入頁按「更換網站 Change site」。
To change it later: tap "Change site" on the sign-in page.
